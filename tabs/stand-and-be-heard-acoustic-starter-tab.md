## Option B: closer to the recording (no capo, barre voicings)

Pass-2 voicing analysis (2026-10-24) suggests the recorded guitar emphasizes **root – 5th – octave on the low strings**, with weak 3rds:
- **A♭:** the strong E♭3 and weak C3 point to the **E-shape barre at the 4th fret**, *not* the open G shape.
- **G♭:** a low G♭2 root, and no major 7th, point to the **full E-shape barre at the 2nd fret**, not Fmaj7.
- **B♭m:** B♭2 / F3 / B♭3 match the **A-shape minor barre at the 1st fret** (identical to capo-1 Am).
- **D♭:** inconclusive (the C-shape and A-shape barre fit about equally).

## Patterns

Count: `1 & 2 & 3 & 4 &`. D = down, U = up, `.` = let ring.

| Code | Pattern | Feel |
|---|---|---|
| **P1** ring | `D . . . . . . .` | one strum per bar, let it ring |
| **P2** halves | `D . . . D . . .` | beats 1 and 3 |
| **P3** quarters | `D . D . D . D .` | steady pulse |
| **P4** folk | `D . D U . U D U` | standard singer-songwriter strum |
| **P5** 16ths | `DUDU DUDU DUDU DUDU` | busy, light wrist (count 1-e-&-a) |
| **P6** break | `D . D . D U D U` | measured 8th pulse in the break |

Arpeggio option for P2/P3 sections: pick the bass note on beat 1, then strings 3-2-1-2 in 8ths.

## Practice order (Thursday)

1. Shapes alone: Am → F → G → C changes, slow, clean. F is the hard one; use Fmaj7 until the barre is there.
2. Chorus loop, bars 90–101 (Studio loop), **P4**, over the backing.
3. Verse 2 loop, bars 49–80, **P3**.
4. Turn + Break, bars 119–133: the only busy strumming.
5. Full run with the backing; then solo `guitar.wav` section by section and adjust the patterns toward what's actually played.
