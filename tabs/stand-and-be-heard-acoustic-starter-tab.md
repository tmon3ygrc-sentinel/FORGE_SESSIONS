# Stand and Be Heard (Acoustic) — starter tab

> **What this is:** a playable *starting arrangement*, not a note-for-note transcription.
> Chords and bars come from the analysis in `stand-and-be-heard-acoustic.md` (high confidence in the strong sections).
> Patterns are **fitted to how busy each section measured** (onsets per bar), because the original part
> isn't grid-locked enough to transcribe automatically (flat timing histogram → loose / fingerpicked feel).
> Refine by ear against `guitar.wav`. Practice over `backing-no-guitar.wav` or Suno Studio track 3.

**Capo 1 (all 7 strings — check your capo covers the 1.98" nut). 139 BPM, 4/4. Sounds in B♭ minor.**
Tab shows fret numbers *relative to the capo* (0 = capo). The 7th string (low B) is **not used**: mute it with the thumb or just don't strum it.

F is a full barre (1st fret above the capo). Easier option: **Fmaj7** = `x x 3 2 1 0` (D-G-B-e: 3-2-1-0). It sounds softer but fits the acoustic style.

## Patterns

Count: `1 & 2 & 3 & 4 &`. D = down, U = up, `.` = let ring.

| Code | Pattern | Feel |
|---|---|---|
| **P1** ring | `D . . . . . . .` | one strum per bar, let it ring |
| **P2** halves | `D . . . D . . .` | beats 1 and 3 |
| **P3** quarters | `D . D . D . D .` | steady pulse |
| **P4** folk | `D . D U . U D U` | standard singer-songwriter strum |
| **P5** 16ths | `DUDU DUDU DUDU DUDU` | busy, light wrist (count 1-e-&-a) |
| **P6** break | `D . D . D U D U` | measured 8th pulse in the break |

Arpeggio option for P2/P3 sections: pick the bass note on beat 1, then strings 3-2-1-2 in 8ths.

## Practice order (Thursday)

1. Shapes alone: Am → F → G → C changes, slow, clean. F is the hard one; use Fmaj7 until the barre is there.
2. Chorus loop, bars 90–101 (Studio loop), **P4**, over the backing.
3. Verse 2 loop, bars 49–80, **P3**.
4. Turn + Break, bars 119–133: the only busy strumming.
5. Full run with the backing; then solo `guitar.wav` section by section and adjust the patterns toward what's actually played.
