# World Building — The Trio

Three personas representing three dimensions of one inner journey:
**the warrior who builds, the discipline that refines, the stillness that follows.**

## The Trio

| Character | Role | Name meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **Tmon3yhope** | The American · The Warrior · The Builder | Artist identity |
| **Koji** | The Japanese · The Discipline · The Tradition | 康治 — peace, harmony, healing |
| **Nagi** | The Japanese · The Heart · The Balance | 凪 — earned stillness after the storm |

## How the roles fit together

The three are not separate characters so much as stages of the same movement:

1. **Tmon3yhope — the warrior who builds.** The drive forward: effort, struggle, construction.
2. **Koji — the discipline that refines.** Tradition and practice that shape raw effort into something true.
3. **Nagi — the stillness that follows.** Not emptiness — the calm a sea reaches only after the storm has passed through it.

## In the music

- **Trio songs** carry all three voices — powerful male lead, Japanese male counterpart, ethereal female harmonies — usually bilingual English/Japanese. See *The First Victory*, *What We Cannot Lose*, *Still Small Voice*.
- **Nagi solo** work is Japanese only, sparse and reverent — see *凪の海に光が降る*.

See [`romaji/`](romaji/README.md) for pronunciation and cultural notes on the Japanese lyrics.
