> After the forge, the fire doesn't go out. It goes acoustic. The Quiet Within takes the songs of Between the Embers and Bushidō down to wood and string: no distortion, no wall of drums, just hands on a guitar, a heartbeat on a cajon, and the words sung at full strength on a porch at sunrise. Some battles are won loud. These are won unplugged, and they're still battles.
