# 凪の海に光が降る

*Nagi no umi ni hikari ga furu — Light descends on a stilled sea*

> Pronunciation key and notes on interpretation: see [README](README.md).

## 1. 握りしめた手をほどけば、風が指の間をぬける。

- **Romaji:** *Nigirishimeta te wo hodokeba, kaze ga yubi no aida wo nukeru.*
- **Pronunciation:** Ni-gi-ri-shi-meh-tah teh wo ho-do-keh-bah, kah-zeh gah yu-bi no ah-ee-dah wo nu-keh-ru
- **Meaning:** If I loosen the hands I've been gripping tight, the wind passes through my fingers.

> 握りしめた (nigirishimeta) = gripped tight, clenched. The past tense implies long-held tension. ほどけば (hodokeba) = conditional release — if I untangle, if I let go. Beautiful physical image of surrender.

## 2. すべてを導かなくていい。道はもう主の手の中。

- **Romaji:** *Subete wo michibika nakute ii. Michi wa mou Shu no te no naka.*
- **Pronunciation:** Su-beh-teh wo mi-chi-bi-kah nah-ku-teh ee. Mi-chi wah moh Shu no teh no nah-kah
- **Meaning:** I don't have to guide everything. The path is already in the Lord's hands.

> 主 (Shu) = the Lord. This is specifically Christian usage. もう (mou) = already — the path is already held. Not eventually, not if we trust enough. Already.

## 3. 手放せば光が満ちる、恐れじゃなく自由のために。

- **Romaji:** *Tebanaseba hikari ga michiru, osore ja naku jiyū no tame ni.*
- **Pronunciation:** Teh-bah-nah-seh-bah hi-kah-ri gah mi-chi-ru, oh-so-reh jah nah-ku ji-yoo no tah-meh ni
- **Meaning:** When I let go, light fills the space — not for fear, but for freedom.

> 自由 (jiyū) = freedom, liberty. The letting go is not motivated by fear of consequences but by desire for freedom. Motivation matters in both Bushidō and Christian theology.

## 4. このあかりは進むための光、信じて前へ。

- **Romaji:** *Kono akari wa susumu tame no hikari, shinjite mae e.*
- **Pronunciation:** Ko-no ah-kah-ri wah su-su-mu tah-meh no hi-kah-ri, shin-ji-teh mah-eh eh
- **Meaning:** This light is the light to move forward by. Trust — and go ahead.

> あかり (akari) = warm light, candlelight — softer than 光 (hikari). Two light words in one line: the warm intimate light and the broader radiance. 信じて (shinjite) = trusting, believing.

## 5. 愛は探してつかむものじゃない、気づけば愛が私を見つけてた。

- **Romaji:** *Ai wa sagashite tsukamu mono ja nai, kidzukeba ai ga watashi wo mitsuketeta.*
- **Pronunciation:** Ah-i wah sah-gah-shi-teh tsu-kah-mu mo-no jah nai, ki-dzu-keh-bah ah-i gah wah-tah-shi wo mi-tsu-keh-teh-tah
- **Meaning:** Love is not something you search for and seize — before I knew it, love had found me.

> つかむ (tsukamu) = to grasp, to seize. Love is not captured — it arrives. 気づけば (kidzukeba) = when I became aware, before I realized. Love preceded the awareness of it.

## 6. 心が揺れる夜には、みことばが舵をとる。

- **Romaji:** *Kokoro ga yureru yoru ni wa, mikotoba ga kaji wo toru.*
- **Pronunciation:** Ko-ko-ro gah yu-reh-ru yo-ru ni wah, mi-ko-to-bah gah kah-ji wo to-ru
- **Meaning:** On the nights my heart trembles, His word takes the helm.

> 揺れる (yureru) = to tremble, sway, shake. みことば (mikotoba) = the Word of God, scripture — honorific prefix み (mi). 舵をとる (kaji wo toru) = to take the helm, to steer. The Word steers when the heart cannot.

## 7. ゆだねたとき限界を越える、主の手が私を支える。

- **Romaji:** *Yudaneta toki genkai wo koeru, Shu no te ga watashi wo sasaeru.*
- **Pronunciation:** Yu-dah-neh-tah to-ki gen-kah-i wo koh-eh-ru, Shu no teh gah wah-tah-shi wo sah-sah-eh-ru
- **Meaning:** When I surrender, I surpass my limits. His hand holds me up.

> ゆだねる (yudaneru) = to entrust, to surrender to another's care. 限界 (genkai) = limit, boundary. Surrender is the condition for transcending limits — not effort, not striving. Surrender.

## 8. 凪の海に光が降る。

- **Romaji:** *Nagi no umi ni hikari ga furu.*
- **Pronunciation:** Nah-gi no u-mi ni hi-kah-ri gah fu-ru
- **Meaning:** Light descends on a stilled sea.

> 凪 (nagi) = the calm after a storm, when the sea becomes glassy and still. Not emptiness — earned stillness. 降る (furu) = to fall, to descend. Light does not rise from within — it descends. The title and thesis of the entire song.

## 9. 前へ。静かに。

- **Romaji:** *Mae e. Shizuka ni.*
- **Pronunciation:** Mah-eh eh. Shi-zu-kah ni.
- **Meaning:** Forward. Quietly.

> The entire song in a few words. Not forward with noise or striving — forward in stillness. The stilled sea moves forward too. It just does so without turbulence.
