# Still Small Voice

*静かに聞け — Listen in stillness*

> Pronunciation key and notes on interpretation: see [README](README.md).

## 1. 静かに聞け。

- **Romaji:** *Shizuka ni kike.*
- **Pronunciation:** Shi-zu-kah ni ki-keh
- **Meaning:** Listen in stillness.

> 静か (shizuka) = quiet, still, peaceful. The command to listen presupposes the discipline to be still first. You cannot hear what you are not quiet enough to receive.

## 2. 神の声に従え。

- **Romaji:** *Kami no koe ni shitagae.*
- **Pronunciation:** Kah-mi no ko-eh ni shi-tah-gah-eh
- **Meaning:** Follow the voice of God.

> 従え (shitagae) = follow, obey, submit to. Not passive compliance but active alignment. The Christian concept of surrender meeting the Japanese concept of discipline.

## 3. ただ神の前に立つ勇気。

- **Romaji:** *Tada kami no mae ni tatsu yūki.*
- **Pronunciation:** Tah-dah kah-mi no mah-eh ni tah-tsu yoo-ki
- **Meaning:** Just the courage to stand before God.

> 勇気 (yūki) = courage, bravery. In Bushidō, courage is not the absence of fear but acting rightly despite it. Standing before God requires the same.

## 4. 己を磨け！恵みの炎で！

- **Romaji:** *Onore wo migake! Megumi no honoo de!*
- **Pronunciation:** Oh-no-reh wo mi-gah-keh! Meh-gu-mi no ho-no-oh deh!
- **Meaning:** Refine yourself! By the fire of grace!

> 磨く (migaku) = to polish, to refine, to sharpen. The same word used for polishing a blade. Grace here is not passive — it is the fire that does the refining work.

## 5. 力は天から降る。

- **Romaji:** *Chikara wa ten kara furu.*
- **Pronunciation:** Chi-kah-rah wah ten kah-rah fu-ru
- **Meaning:** Strength descends from heaven.

> 天 (ten) = heaven, sky, the divine above. 降る (furu) = to fall, to descend. Strength is not generated within — it is received from above and carried through the person.

## 6. 静かに。御前に。共に。

- **Romaji:** *Shizuka ni. Mimae ni. Tomo ni.*
- **Pronunciation:** Shi-zu-kah ni. Mi-mah-eh ni. Toh-mo ni.
- **Meaning:** In stillness. Before Him. Together.

> 御前に (mimae ni) in this sacred context = before God / in His presence. Three postures that define the song: stillness, humility before God, and community.
