# What We Cannot Lose

*手放せば光が満ちる — When I let go, light fills the space*

> Pronunciation key and notes on interpretation: see [README](README.md).

## 1. 手放せ。

- **Romaji:** *Tebanase.*
- **Pronunciation:** Teh-bah-nah-seh
- **Meaning:** Release it.

> 手放す (tebanasu) = to release from the hand. Physical, tactile letting go. More visceral than simply "let go."

## 2. 前へ進め。

- **Romaji:** *Mae e susume.*
- **Pronunciation:** Mah-eh eh su-su-meh
- **Meaning:** Move forward.

> 進め (susume) = advance, press forward. Military bearing. A command to move, not a suggestion.

## 3. また立ち上がれ！知らぬ彼方へ！

- **Romaji:** *Mata tachiagare! Shiranu kanata e!*
- **Pronunciation:** Mah-tah tah-chi-ah-gah-reh! Shi-rah-nu kah-nah-tah eh!
- **Meaning:** Rise again! Toward the unknown horizon!

> 彼方 (kanata) = distant place, far beyond. 知らぬ = unknown, unfamiliar. Rising toward what cannot yet be seen.

## 4. 手放すことで得られるものがある。

- **Romaji:** *Tebanasu koto de erareru mono ga aru.*
- **Pronunciation:** Teh-bah-nah-su ko-to deh eh-rah-reh-ru mo-no gah ah-ru
- **Meaning:** In letting go there is something to be gained.

> The paradox at the heart of the song — surrender is not loss, it is the condition for receiving what actually belongs to you.

## 5. 古いものを手放せ！恐れを砕け！

- **Romaji:** *Furui mono wo tebanase! Osore wo kudake!*
- **Pronunciation:** Fu-ru-i mo-no wo teh-bah-nah-seh! Oh-so-reh wo ku-dah-keh!
- **Meaning:** Release the old! Shatter the fear!

> 砕け (kudake) = shatter, break apart. More violent than simply releasing — fear is not gently let go, it is broken.

## 6. 永遠のものを抱け！

- **Romaji:** *Eien no mono wo idake!*
- **Pronunciation:** Eh-ee-en no mo-no wo ee-dah-keh!
- **Meaning:** Hold what endures!

> 抱く (idaku) = to embrace, to hold close. 永遠 (eien) = eternal, forever. What is eternal is worth holding. What is temporary must be released.

## 7. 前へ。共に。

- **Romaji:** *Mae e. Tomo ni.*
- **Pronunciation:** Mah-eh eh. Toh-mo ni.
- **Meaning:** Forward. Together.

> The song distills to a few words. Direction and relationship — the two things that matter when everything else is released.
