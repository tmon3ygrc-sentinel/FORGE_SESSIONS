# The First Victory

*己に勝て — Conquer the Self*

> Pronunciation key and notes on interpretation: see [README](README.md).

## 1. 己を知れ。

- **Romaji:** *Onore wo shire.*
- **Pronunciation:** Oh-no-reh wo shi-reh
- **Meaning:** Know yourself.

> 己 (onore) is classical/formal "self" — samurai register, not casual speech. Direct command from sensei to student.

## 2. 己に勝て。

- **Romaji:** *Onore ni kate.*
- **Pronunciation:** Oh-no-reh ni kah-teh
- **Meaning:** Conquer yourself.

> Core of Masakatsu Agatsu — true victory is victory over the self. Foundation of Aikidō teaching from O Sensei Morihei Ueshiba.

## 3. 勝つことだけが勝利じゃない。

- **Romaji:** *Katsu koto dake ga shōri ja nai.*
- **Pronunciation:** Kah-tsu ko-to dah-keh gah show-ri jah nai
- **Meaning:** Winning alone is not true victory.

> だけ (dake) = only/alone. Challenges hollow achievement. Victory without honor or service is empty.

## 4. 自分を越えて、誰かを生かす。

- **Romaji:** *Jibun wo koete, dareka wo ikasu.*
- **Pronunciation:** Ji-bun wo koh-eh-teh, dah-reh-kah wo ee-kah-su
- **Meaning:** Surpass yourself — give life to someone else.

> 生かす (ikasu) = to make someone come alive, not merely help. Connected to Ikigai — purpose directed outward toward others.

## 5. 己に勝て！そして共に立て！

- **Romaji:** *Onore ni kate! Soshite tomo ni tate!*
- **Pronunciation:** Oh-no-reh ni kah-teh! So-shi-teh toh-mo ni tah-teh!
- **Meaning:** Conquer yourself! Then rise together!

> そして (soshite) = sequential "and then." Internal victory first, collective rising second. The order is intentional.

## 6. 刃は心を映す鏡、恐れも迷いも隠せない。

- **Romaji:** *Yaiba wa kokoro wo utsusu kagami, osore mo mayoi mo kakusenai.*
- **Pronunciation:** Yah-ee-bah wah ko-ko-ro wo oo-tsu-su kah-gah-mi, oh-so-reh mo mah-yoi mo kah-ku-seh-nai
- **Meaning:** The blade reflects the heart — fear and hesitation cannot be hidden.

> 迷い (mayoi) = hesitation/being lost. In Bushidō, hesitation is more dangerous than fear. A skilled opponent reads your state before you strike.

## 7. 城を守る者よ、まず己の門を守れ。

- **Romaji:** *Shiro wo mamoru mono yo, mazu onore no mon wo mamore.*
- **Pronunciation:** Shi-ro wo mah-mo-ru mo-no yo, mah-zu oh-no-reh no mon wo mah-mo-reh
- **Meaning:** Guardian of the castle — first guard your own gate.

> よ (yo) = classical vocative, addressing with weight. 門 (mon) = gate/threshold. The inner gate is always the first to defend.

## 8. 欲を手放し、誇りを捨て。

- **Romaji:** *Yoku wo tebanashi, hokori wo sute.*
- **Pronunciation:** Yo-ku wo teh-bah-nah-shi, ho-ko-ri wo su-teh
- **Meaning:** Release desire — cast away pride.

> 手放し (tebanashi) = physical release from the hand. 捨て (sute) = decisive discard. Both Bushidō and Zen name attachment to outcome and reputation as the path to defeat.

## 9. 残るものこそ、真の力。

- **Romaji:** *Nokoru mono koso, shin no chikara.*
- **Pronunciation:** No-ko-ru mo-no ko-so, shin no chi-kah-rah
- **Meaning:** What remains is the true strength.

> こそ (koso) = emphatic "that and nothing else." After desire and pride are removed what remains is essence — the concept of Mu (無), the productive void.

## 10. 刃を収めろ。

- **Romaji:** *Yaiba wo osamero.*
- **Pronunciation:** Yah-ee-bah wo oh-sah-meh-ro
- **Meaning:** Return the blade to its sheath.

> The highest Bushidō mastery. Choosing not to strike when you could. The sheathed sword is the strongest statement a warrior makes.

## 11. 一人の勝利ではない。一つの火を分け合う。

- **Romaji:** *Hitori no shōri de wa nai. Hitotsu no hi wo wakeau.*
- **Pronunciation:** Hi-to-ri no show-ri deh wah nai. Hi-to-tsu no hi wo wah-keh-ah-u
- **Meaning:** It is not one person's victory. We share one fire.

> 分け合う (wakeau) = to share between each other. One flame lighting another without diminishing. Connected to On (恩) — gratitude and obligation flowing between people.

## 12. 勝利は終わりではない。

- **Romaji:** *Shōri wa owari de wa nai.*
- **Pronunciation:** Show-ri wah oh-wah-ri deh wah nai
- **Meaning:** Victory is not the ending.

> The entire song's thesis in one line. Victory is a threshold, not a destination.

## 13. それは次の命へ渡す火だ。

- **Romaji:** *Sore wa tsugi no inochi e watasu hi da.*
- **Pronunciation:** So-reh wah tsu-gi no ee-no-chi eh wah-tah-su hi dah
- **Meaning:** It is the fire we pass to the next life.

> 命 (inochi) = life, carrying the full meaning and spirit of existence. 渡す (watasu) = to hand over, to pass across. The warrior's final purpose.

## 14. 共に。

- **Romaji:** *Tomo ni.*
- **Pronunciation:** Toh-mo ni
- **Meaning:** Together.

> The entire song in a few words. 共 (tomo) = together, shared, companion. に (ni) = directional — moving toward something together. Individual meaning is incomplete without the collective.
