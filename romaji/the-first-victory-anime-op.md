# The First Victory (Anime OP)

*最初の勝利は己に勝つこと — The first victory is conquering yourself*

> Pronunciation key and notes on interpretation: see [README](README.md).
> This version keeps all 14 Japanese lines of the original — see [The First Victory](the-first-victory.md) for those. Only the lines new to this version are listed here, in the order they are sung.

## 1. その力を　誰かのために。

- **Romaji:** *Sono chikara wo dareka no tame ni.*
- **Pronunciation:** So-no chi-kah-rah wo dah-reh-kah no tah-meh ni
- **Meaning:** That strength — for someone else.

> のために (no tame ni) = for the sake of. The strength won in the battle within is immediately turned outward.

## 2. 一人が立てば、次の者も立てる。

- **Romaji:** *Hitori ga tateba, tsugi no mono mo tateru.*
- **Pronunciation:** Hi-to-ri gah tah-teh-bah, tsu-gi no mo-no mo tah-teh-ru
- **Meaning:** When one stands, the next can stand too.

> 立てば (tateba) = "when/if one stands." 立てる (tateru) = "is able to stand." One person rising is what makes the next person's rising possible.

## 3. 一つの命が　多くの命を照らす。

- **Romaji:** *Hitotsu no inochi ga ōku no inochi wo terasu.*
- **Pronunciation:** Hi-to-tsu no ee-no-chi gah oh-ku no ee-no-chi wo teh-rah-su
- **Meaning:** One life lights up many lives.

> 照らす (terasu) = to shine on, to illuminate. The single shared fire from the line before becomes light for a whole village.

## 4. 王座を捨てろ！

- **Romaji:** *Ōza wo sutero!*
- **Pronunciation:** Oh-zah wo su-teh-ro!
- **Meaning:** Cast away the throne!

> 王座 (ōza) = throne. The English shout is "crown" (王冠, ōkan); the throne also echoes "Pride on the throne" from verse 2. 捨てろ (sutero) is the blunt command form — harsher than the 捨て of verse 2.

## 5. 一つの心！一つの道！

- **Romaji:** *Hitotsu no kokoro! Hitotsu no michi!*
- **Pronunciation:** Hi-to-tsu no ko-ko-ro! Hi-to-tsu no mi-chi!
- **Meaning:** One heart! One path!

> Answers the English "ONE SWORD! ONE HEART!" 道 (michi) = path, and also "the Way" — as in 武士道 (Bushidō) and 武道 (budō).

## 6. 一つの火！共に立て！

- **Romaji:** *Hitotsu no hi! Tomo ni tate!*
- **Pronunciation:** Hi-to-tsu no hi! Toh-mo ni tah-teh!
- **Meaning:** One fire! Stand together!

> Pulls together two earlier lines: 一つの火を分け合う (we share one fire) and 共に立て (rise together).

## 7. その種を大地へ返せ。

- **Romaji:** *Sono tane wo daichi e kaese.*
- **Pronunciation:** So-no tah-neh wo dai-chi eh kah-eh-seh
- **Meaning:** Return that seed to the earth.

> 返せ (kaese) = give back. A victory is not kept — it is planted, so something can grow from it.

## 8. その知恵を次の者へ。

- **Romaji:** *Sono chie wo tsugi no mono e.*
- **Pronunciation:** So-no chi-eh wo tsu-gi no mo-no eh
- **Meaning:** Pass that wisdom to the one who comes next.

> The verb is left unsaid after へ (e, "toward") — a common lyrical ellipsis; "hand it on" is understood.

## 9. 橋を渡り、共に進め。

- **Romaji:** *Hashi wo watari, tomo ni susume.*
- **Pronunciation:** Hah-shi wo wah-tah-ri, toh-mo ni su-su-meh
- **Meaning:** Cross the bridge, and move forward together.

> 渡る (wataru) = to cross over. The same verb as 渡す (watasu, to hand over) in the outro: crossing and passing on are one movement.

## 10. 最初の勝利は己に勝つこと！

- **Romaji:** *Saisho no shōri wa onore ni katsu koto!*
- **Pronunciation:** Sai-sho no show-ri wah oh-no-reh ni kah-tsu ko-to!
- **Meaning:** The first victory is conquering yourself!

> The song's title, in Japanese. こと (koto) turns 己に勝つ (conquer yourself) into a noun: "the act of conquering yourself."

## 11. 共に生きろ！

- **Romaji:** *Tomo ni ikiro!*
- **Pronunciation:** Toh-mo ni ee-ki-ro!
- **Meaning:** Live together!

> Sung against "WE GROW TO GIVE!" — growing is for the sake of living alongside others.

## 12. 己を超えろ！

- **Romaji:** *Onore wo koero!*
- **Pronunciation:** Oh-no-reh wo koh-eh-ro!
- **Meaning:** Go beyond yourself!

> 超える (koeru) = to exceed a limit. The pre-chorus's 自分を越えて uses 越える — same reading, "to cross over." Either spelling is correct.

## 13. 共に進め！

- **Romaji:** *Tomo ni susume!*
- **Pronunciation:** Toh-mo ni su-su-meh!
- **Meaning:** Move forward together!

> The last of three commands in the final chorus: conquer, go beyond, move forward — each one answered in English by WE RISE, WE BECOME, WE SERVE.

## 14. 誇りを捨てろ！

- **Romaji:** *Hokori wo sutero!*
- **Pronunciation:** Ho-ko-ri wo su-teh-ro!
- **Meaning:** Cast away pride!

> Sung in the final gang-vocal bridge against "RELEASE THE EGO!" — the command version of verse 2's 誇りを捨て. Here 誇り means pride as ego — the thing that sits on the throne.

## 15. 火を受け継げ！

- **Romaji:** *Hi wo uketsuge!*
- **Pronunciation:** Hi wo u-keh-tsu-geh!
- **Meaning:** Carry on the fire!

> 受け継ぐ (uketsugu) = to receive something and carry it forward — an inheritance. It sets up the outro: それは次の命へ渡す火だ (it is the fire we pass to the next life).
